## Claims that are safe to publish

- Read-only Robinhood Chain connection is live.
- Asset Passport checks chain evidence and official registries.
- Policy checks cover transaction value, daily spend, memecoin exposure, reserves, slippage, canonical RWA contracts, trading halts, quote freshness, risk signals, and allowlists.
- Human approval is bound to an immutable plan.
- Receipts are tamper-evident and keep prompts/private memory offchain.
- Broadcasting is disabled in the public preview.

Do not claim guaranteed safety, guaranteed returns, legal ownership of an underlying RWA, automated mainnet trading, or an audited/deployed receipt contract.
