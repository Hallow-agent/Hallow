It is a local operating layer and standard around agents. It can sit above orchestration frameworks and organize packages, memory, tools, routes, policies, and evidence.

The current catalog supports OpenAI-compatible APIs, Claude-style providers, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, local Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, and fallback profiles.
