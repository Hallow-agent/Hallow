The Guardian sequence

Five gates between intent and consequence.

01 / OBSERVE

Asset Passport

Read bytecode, metadata, block height, canonical registries, and current market-state signals.

02 / EXPLAIN

Human meaning

Translate evidence into limits and uncertainty. Never call a token “safe.”

03 / SIMULATE

Hard policy

Spending, reserve, slippage, exposure, allowlists, halts, and stale data can block the plan.

04 / APPROVE

Exact consent

Approval belongs to one immutable plan, not an open-ended agent permission.

05 / PROVE

Receipt hash

Verify the plan and approval without exposing the prompt, private memory, or wallet secret.

A claim we can defend

Not an auto-trader.
A control plane.

Guardian does not promise profit and does not hide risk behind AI confidence. Its first release intentionally ends at approval and proof.

Hard boundary

Broadcast does not exist.

The public preview has read, inspect, plan, approve, and receipt capabilities. Its execute tool is registered at the highest risk level and denied.

RWA awareness

Rights are not assumed.

A registry match is evidence of a canonical deployment. It does not mean ownership of an underlying security, legal eligibility, or guaranteed liquidity.

Memecoin discipline

Exposure has a ceiling.

Guardian can block concentration, high slippage, low reserve, risky bytecode signals, stale quotes, and contracts outside an allowlist.

Privacy boundary

Proof, not private data.

The optional registry accepts hashes only. No prompt, conversation, private memory, seed phrase, or token custody.
