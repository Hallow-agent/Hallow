The Guardian method

Five gates between intent and consequence.

01 / OBSERVE

Build the Asset Passport.

Resolve official identity when available, then capture block height, bytecode, liquidity, volume, holders, and protocol readiness.

02 / EXPLAIN

Separate evidence from inference.

The model may summarize sources and uncertainty. It cannot redefine the contract, invent rights, or upgrade confidence into truth.

03 / SIMULATE

Apply hard policy.

Spend, reserve, slippage, exposure, allowlists, stale data, and emergency halts can reject a plan before approval.

04 / APPROVE

Ask for exact consent.

One approval authorizes one immutable plan. It is not an open-ended permission for the agent to improvise.

05 / PROVE

Return a verifiable receipt.

Hash-derived proof can verify the plan and approval while prompts, private memory, wallet secrets, and personal data stay private.

Claims we can defend

Useful because
it has boundaries.

Guardian is not marketed as an auto-trader, oracle, custodian, or promise of profit. Its value is the control layer between analysis and consequence.

Execution boundary

Broadcast does not exist.

The preview supports inspection, planning, approval, and receipts. No public demo transaction can be broadcast.

RWA boundary

Rights are never assumed.

A canonical deployment does not prove ownership rights, legal eligibility, redemption, or future liquidity.

Memecoin boundary

A ticker inherits no trust.

Arbitrary contracts remain unverified until evidence says otherwise. Thin liquidity and concentration can force a stop.

Privacy boundary

Proof excludes private content.

Receipts use derived hashes. Prompts, conversations, private memory, seed phrases, and custody stay outside public proof.
