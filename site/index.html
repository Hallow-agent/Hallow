001

Hallow is a local-first agent operating system: a private foundation for autonomous work, not another cloud dashboard that owns the context.

Designed around trust

Your machine.
Your memory.
Your agents.

A small surface with a serious runtime underneath. Every action leaves evidence. Every capability has a boundary.

01

Private by architecture

State begins locally. Memory, traces, credentials, approvals, and workspace artifacts stay inside the Hallow home you control.

02

Built to be inspected

Readiness reports, security audits, trace evidence, package signatures, and permission queues turn agent behavior into something visible.
